## 1. PHASE VALIDATION

### Is the US Economy in the "Overheat" Phase?

**Verdict: Confirmed — The U.S. economy is unambiguously in the Overheat phase (47.4°), defined by above-trend real economic growth accompanied by persistent, above-target inflation.**

Under the classical Merrill Lynch Investment Clock framework:
- **Recovery:** Growth > Trend, Inflation < Trend / Target (Quadrant IV: 270° to 360°/0°)
- **Overheat:** Growth > Trend, Inflation > Trend / Target (Quadrant I: 0° to 90°)
- **Stagflation:** Growth < Trend, Inflation > Trend / Target (Quadrant II: 90° to 180°)
- **Reflation:** Growth < Trend, Inflation < Trend / Target (Quadrant III: 180° to 270°)

With the **Composite Growth Z-score at +0.876** and the **Composite Inflation Z-score at +0.952**, both dimensions sit deep in expansionary/inflationary territory ($Z > 0$):
$$\theta = \arctan2(\text{Inflation Z}, \text{Growth Z}) = \arctan2(0.952, 0.876) \approx 47.38^\circ \approx 47.4^\circ$$

Positioned at 47.4°, the economic clock hand sits directly in the active center of Quadrant I (approximately the 1:35 clock position), advancing steadily along the Overheat arc toward the 90° boundary (Stagflation).
