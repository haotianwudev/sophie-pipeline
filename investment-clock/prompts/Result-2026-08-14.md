## **Theoretical Foundations and the Investment Clock Framework**

The Merrill Lynch Investment Clock framework maps macroeconomic cycles across two orthogonal axes: the **Growth Gap** (output relative to potential) and the **Inflation Trend** (price pressures relative to target). The framework identifies four canonical phases:

1. **Reflation** (Growth < Trend, Inflation < Trend): Government Bonds outperform as central banks aggressively ease policy.
2. **Recovery** (Growth > Trend, Inflation < Trend): Equities outperform as corporate profit margins expand amid rising output and low cost pressure.
3. **Overheat** (Growth > Trend, Inflation > Trend): Commodities outperform as capacity constraints emerge and central banks tighten rates.
4. **Stagflation** (Growth < Trend, Inflation > Trend): Cash outperforms as growth contracts while inflation remains sticky.

As of August 14, 2026, the algorithmic evaluation places the U.S. economy at **295.8 degrees** within the **Recovery** phase. The Growth Z-score of **+0.207** signals robust expansion, while the Inflation Z-score of **-0.427** remains negative but is closing rapidly toward the 300-degree Overheat threshold (where Inflation Z crosses 0.0).
